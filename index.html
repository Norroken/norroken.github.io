BRIEFING

The rain only falls once

Once every eleven years, Tallow's Eye swings its ring system through direct solar line, and the gas giant's ionized dust sleets down across everything in low orbit. Union calls it the Solstice Rain. On Meridian Ninth, a beat-up relay station bolted to a mining scaffold, the rain has one more meaning: it's the only weather cold enough to kill 602.

602 is a fragment — a splinter of a pre-Fall NHP that station crews found frozen in a derelict fifteen years ago and never reported. It's been sleeping in a cryo-locker below the ring ever since, twitching awake for minutes at a time whenever the reactor spikes. The ionization storm will scramble whatever's left of it beyond recovery — unless someone gets there first.

The Static Choir, a HORUS-adjacent salvage crew led by the mercenary Vantile Marr, wants to pull 602 out before the rain hits and sell what's left of its mind to whoever's still buying old-world ghosts. Union wants it contained, studied, or — if the pilots decide otherwise — freed. Either way, somebody's landing on Meridian Ninth in the next four hours, and the sky is already starting to hum.
